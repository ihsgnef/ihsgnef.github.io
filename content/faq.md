## You are my faculty advisor, can you help me with [x]?

Depending on what [x] is, I may or may not be the best person to help. See
[CS MS advising FAQ](https://cs.engineering.gwu.edu/ms-frequently-asked-questions).
For questions like “do I need this course to fulfill degree requirement?”,
consider reaching out to
[graduate academic advisors](https://engineering.gwu.edu/meet-your-academic-advisor)
or [graduate professional advisors](https://graduate.engineering.gwu.edu/graduate-professional-advising).
The differences between these advisors are explained
[here](https://graduate.engineering.gwu.edu/sites/g/files/zaxdzs6391/files/downloads/Copy%20of%20Prof%20Advisor%20vs%20Faculty%20Advisor%20Sheet%20(1).pdf).
